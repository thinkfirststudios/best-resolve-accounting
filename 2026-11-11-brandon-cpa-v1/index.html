How it works

Whether you need one tax return or someone to keep your books every month, it starts the same way.

Book a consultation
  1. 1

    Book a consultation

    Call or text (619) 854-1434, or use the form at the bottom of this page. Tell Brandon what you need: a tax return, books that have fallen behind, or a question about your business.

    You get a conversation with a licensed CPA.

  2. 2

    We review your finances

    Brandon looks at where things stand: last year's return, your books or bank statements, and how your income comes in. If your books are behind, he'll tell you, and what catching up would cost.

    You get an honest read on where you stand.

  3. 3

    You get a plan

    He recommends what fits: a tax return, one of the three monthly plans, a catch-up first, or a combination. The price comes from the list on this page.

    You get a clear plan and its price before any work starts.

  4. 4

    Ongoing support

    On a monthly plan, your books stay current, with a Profit and Loss statement and Balance Sheet every month. You can reach him by email, text or phone all year.

    You get an accountant who is still around after April.

Prices

Each monthly plan includes everything in the plan above it. Every tax return includes federal and state filing.

Monthly bookkeeping

For businesses that want their books handled every month.

  • Essentials$395 per month

    For sole proprietors, freelancers and small businesses. A Profit and Loss statement and Balance Sheet every month, and access to your accountant by email, text or phone all year.

  • Professional$595 per month

    For sole proprietors who have grown past the early stages. Adds payroll support, 1099 preparation and issuance, and quarterly check-in calls to review your financials.

  • Elite$895 per month

    For established corporations and partnerships. Adds two tax planning and estimation sessions a year, at mid-year and before year-end.

  • Bookkeeping catch-upfrom $995

    One-time. Past transactions worked through and accounts reconciled, whether you're a few months behind or a few years, so a monthly plan can start from accurate books.

Tax returns

Priced by how much is on the return.

  • Simple$155–$225

    W-2 income only, without itemized deductions.

  • Personal$250–$450

    W-2 income plus stock, crypto, interest or dividends, or if you itemize.

  • Sole proprietorfrom $495

    Independent contractors and sole proprietors, including rental property or a mix of W-2 and investment income.

  • Businessfrom $1,095

    Partnerships, S-Corporations and C-Corporations, including Form K-1s for partners and shareholders.
