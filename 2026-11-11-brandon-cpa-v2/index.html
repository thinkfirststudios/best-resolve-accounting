Monthly plans

What each plan includes, every month

Bookkeeping is one of the most overlooked tax strategies. When your books are clean and current all year, every deductible expense gets captured: a smoother filing season, fewer surprises, and more money in your pocket.

  • Essentials

    $395 / month

    For sole proprietors, freelancers and small business owners.

    • Profit & Loss statement every month
    • Balance Sheet every month
    • Year-round access to your accountant by email, text or phone

  • Professional

    $595 / month
