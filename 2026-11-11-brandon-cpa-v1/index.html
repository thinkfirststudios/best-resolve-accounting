How it works

Four steps from first call to clear books

You'll know what happens after you reach out, what it costs, and who is doing the work. It's the same path whether you need one tax return or bookkeeping every month.

  1. 1

    Book a consultation

    Call or text (619) 854-1434, or send the form below. Tell us what's going on: a tax return, books that have fallen behind, or a question about your business.

    You get: a direct conversation with a licensed CPA.

  2. 2

    We review your finances

    We look at where things stand: last year's return, your books or bank statements, and how your income comes in. If your books are behind, you'll hear that up front.

    You get: an honest picture of where you stand.

  3. 3

    You get a plan

    We recommend the right fit: a tax return, a monthly plan (Essentials, Professional or Elite), a catch-up first, or a mix. Every option is priced on this page.

    You get: a clear recommendation and the price before work begins.

  4. 4

    Ongoing support

    On a monthly plan, your books stay current with a Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet every month. Questions outside tax season? Reach us by email, text or phone.

    You get: an accountant who doesn't disappear after April.
