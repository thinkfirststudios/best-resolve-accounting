Who we are

Led by Brandon Rodriguez, a licensed CPA in San Diego. In business since 2019, and a one-person firm: the CPA you talk to is the one doing your work.

Best Resolve Accounting provides personalized tax and accounting services, and with close to a decade of experience, we've built a practice that goes well beyond the basics.

We work with a wide range of clients, from simple individual tax filers to business owners across industries including Real Estate, E-Commerce, Engineering, Content Creators, Automotive, Therapy, and Food & Beverage. That breadth of experience isn't just a list, it means we bring real world insight from across industries into every client relationship, so no matter what your business looks like, we've likely seen it, solved it, and know how to make it work in your favor.

From bookkeeping and payroll to tax strategy and business consulting, we're your trusted advisor for every financial challenge your business faces.

How it works

From a free call to a plan that fits

You'll know what happens after you reach out, what it costs, and who is doing the work: Brandon, every step. It's the same path whether you need one tax return or bookkeeping every month.

  1. 01

    Book a free consultation

    Call or text (619) 854-1434, or send the form below. Tell Brandon what's going on: a tax return, books that have fallen behind, or a question about your business.

    You get: a free call with a licensed CPA.

  2. 02

    He learns your business

    Brandon gets to know your business and works out exactly what stage it's at: where your books stand and how your income comes in. If your books are behind, you'll hear that up front.

    You get: an honest picture of where you stand.

  3. 03

    A plan tailored to you

    An accounting and tax plan built around your stage: a monthly bookkeeping plan, a catch-up first, tax filing, or a mix. Bookkeeping plans and tax returns are priced on this page.

    You get: a clear recommendation and the price before work begins.

  4. 04

    A direct line, all year

    On a monthly plan, your books stay current with a Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet every month. Questions go straight to Brandon by email, text or phone.

    You get: your CPA, never a hand-off.
