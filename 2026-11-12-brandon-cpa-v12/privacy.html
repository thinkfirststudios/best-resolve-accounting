Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information Best Resolve Accounting ("we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website only. If you become a client, how we handle your tax and financial records is set out in your engagement letter and our client privacy notice.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you send the contact form, we receive your first and last name, email address and phone number, plus anything optional you choose to add: your business name, how you heard about us, and your questions or comments. If you call, text or email us, we receive whatever you choose to include.

Information collected automatically. Like any website, our hosting provider records basic technical information when pages load, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We use it only to keep the site running and secure.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies on this website.

Please don't send Social Security numbers, bank or account numbers, or tax documents through the form or by regular email. We'll arrange a secure way to share them if you become a client.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and set up your free consultation.
  • To answer questions about our services and prices.
  • To keep the website working and protect it from spam and abuse.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Who else handles it

We use a few service providers to run this website. They handle information only to provide their service to us:

  • Netlify hosts the website and receives the contact form, then emails it to us.
  • Google delivers our email (Gmail) and the fonts used on this site. If you book through our scheduling page, Google Calendar handles that booking under Google's own privacy policy.

Links to our Google reviews, Instagram and Facebook pages take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form messages only as long as we need them to respond and follow up, and we delete them from our form service within 12 months. If you become a client, your records are kept as your engagement letter and professional rules require.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information you sent through this website. Email bestresolveaccntng@gmail.com or call (619) 854-1434, and we'll respond within 30 days.

Do Not Track. This website doesn't track you across other sites, so it treats every visit the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information you send us, and the site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure.

Children

This website is meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
